APE PARA (අපේ පාර) - LOGO PACK
==============================

FILES (this folder = vector sources)
  ape-para-logo.svg             transparent, for light backgrounds
  ape-para-logo-on-maroon.svg   on deep maroon, for dark backgrounds / social profile
  ape-para-logo-horizontal.svg  for wide spaces such as headers (with tagline)
  ape-para-icon.svg             app icon / favicon
  Text is converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed to use these files.
  The app's own copies (compact header lockup, PNG icons, favicon.ico) are in frontend/public/.

CONCEPT
  A moonstone (sandakada pahana): the carved semicircular stone laid at the threshold of ancient Sri Lankan
  buildings. Here the lotus-petal ring and the row of dots echo its carved bands. At its centre a road runs
  to a map pin: the doorway to getting your road fixed. It is inspired by the moonstone tradition, not a copy
  of any specific stone.

USAGE
  - Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the pin.
  - Don't stretch, recolour the rings, or place the light version on busy photos.
  - Minimum size: emblem 48 px wide; icon 32 px.
